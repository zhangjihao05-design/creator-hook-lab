Open by design

Hook Lab is an open agentic website. Its agent network is public JSON (hook_lab.network.json, listed in agent-manifest.json). Other agentic websites can call it at this-site/hook_lab and get ranked hooks for their own visitors. It links with Creator Agent Studio, which turns a topic into a brief, a YouTube script, an X thread and a storyboard.

Scores are an AI critic's judgment, not audience data. Hook Lab does not post, research trends or read analytics.
